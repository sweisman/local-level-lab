# Data license

All session data contributed to Local Level Lab, meaning the session zips (the raw sensor streams and manifests) and their index, is dedicated to the public domain under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Contributors agree to this when they upload, and every manifest records `"data_license": "CC0-1.0"`. Anyone may download, reanalyze, republish or criticise the data for any purpose, without asking.

The software (app, server, analysis) is licensed separately under the GNU AGPL v3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

The bundled [OpenFlights airline directory](https://openflights.org/data.php) is separately
licensed under **ODbL-1.0**. Its [README](android/app/src/main/assets/README.md) records provenance
and attribution, with the upstream license alongside it. It is not part of the CC0 recording
dataset. Third-party flight tracks retain their source terms too; supplying a track for checking
or recovery does not make it CC0 through the session's license declaration. Preserve source
and provenance separately.
