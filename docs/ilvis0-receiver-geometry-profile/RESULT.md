# Conditional contrast geometry in longer tracks

The fixed study closed 29 preselected candidate tasks. 29 produced complete conditional pairwise profiles; the other tasks remain in the [file ledger](files.csv). No recorded gyro residual or Earth-model fit was used.

The [pairwise table](geometry.csv) lists all three model pairs under constant bias, bias plus drift, and the wider local gain/mount tangent treatment. The reported retention is the worst over the declared crab, roll, pitch and model anchors. It is a fraction of a **predicted rate contrast**, not detection power or a test result.

Median retained fractions under the widest local nuisance treatment are: `sphere_rotating|flat_still` 1.427e-09, `sphere_rotating|sphere_still` 3.834e-09, `sphere_still|flat_still` 1.258e-09.

**Aircraft rotation is assumed independently removable in this geometry calculation.** The available receiver fixes do not supply body attitude or a calibrated error law. With unrestricted aircraft rotation, all these rate contrasts can be absorbed. A favorable table entry therefore cannot promote a track to scientific eligibility. The original 53-stretch study and reserved ESA groups remain unchanged.
