# Qualify prediction ranges over recording lengths

The short-example width gate is complete: all 48 regions and six case covers
pass. Never resume this study or reuse its unused allowance.

The [four-minute input adapter](../ilvis0-recording-adapter-controls/README.md)
is complete. The separate [50-second growth screen](../ilvis0-recording-growth-screen/INTERPRETATION.md)
completes all six cases, measuring 312–520 seconds per bound and 71–72 km
unconstrained outer radii. Its smaller approximation remainder does not give a
narrower full range than the two available historical whole-change bounds.
Preserve those complementary constraints when preparing training-conditioned
predictions; neither kind of unconstrained range is useful by itself.
The [explicit-allowance interface](../ilvis0-recording-training-interface/INTERPRETATION.md)
is checked for later training constraints; its caller must supply a justified
error budget instead of inheriting the short example's 1 mm allowance.

Qualification must cover the entire integration history, not only the unused
prediction period. The earlier representative prediction plan spans 438 seconds
in total, with 292 training epochs and 147 later epochs. The four-minute inputs
used for compatibility and cost checks cannot by themselves qualify that full
horizon. Freeze the intended histories and their complete clock support before
the next recording-length study; preserve the existing fixed-clock inputs.

1. Inspect the [earlier four-minute study](../ilvis0-recording-scale-bounds/INTERPRETATION.md)
   and its frozen inputs, outcomes and mathematical assumptions. Preserve its
   incomplete cases and broad/narrow sensitivity outcomes unchanged.
2. Define a recording-length feasibility study for the current shared-calibration
   method, retaining all candidates and processing assumptions. Rebuild source
   bindings and derive any training-feasible restrictions from the new study's
   own training inputs. Short-history certificates cannot be silently relabeled
   for a longer history. Preserve every physical direction and clock-support
   requirement.
3. Cost the work from measured source visits before launch. A short, bounded
   duration/runtime screen may precede expensive complete-record calculations,
   but it cannot qualify recording-length usefulness. Freeze all query times,
   domains, input hashes, runtime limits and acceptance rules separately.
   Bound-source and clock reports are query-time dependent. Reusing an earlier
   integration state at another query time requires separate qualification;
   streaming value/Jacobian equivalence alone does not establish bound reuse.
4. Require complete coverage, supported numerical treatment and useful ranges
   over the declared recording/prediction horizon. Retaining known truth alone
   is insufficient; enormous conservative ranges do not pass usefulness.
5. Apply the [explicit error assumptions](../PREDICTION_ERROR_ASSUMPTIONS.md).
   Account for receiver error and accumulated inertial noise, or justify any
   negligible contribution. Assess a shared error-law family without letting
   a poorly fitting candidate enlarge its own uncertainty or use future data.

V01 remains in progress until those requirements are met. New withheld
prediction fits remain V02 work and have not started. Every new study requires
its own finite freeze; no completed budget, protected ESA outcome or aircraft
measurement is available for an automatic retry or rollout.
