# Saved two-start forecast agreement

The original 80-fit development study contains 40 matched training-cut pairs across 20 recording groups. 30/80 individual fits passed their original stopping checks.

40/40 saved pairs have a maximum withheld-path difference within 0.1 m. The largest pair difference is 0.00603 m. The threshold comes from generated solver development and is a diagnostic here, not a calibrated aircraft acceptance rule.

Both forecasts in each pair were saved before later receiver positions were scored. This compares predicted paths from two starting points on the same observations. Close agreement does not prove either fit is an optimum, accurate on withheld positions, or physically adequate. The original 50 nonpassing labels remain unchanged. No native calls or new aircraft fits were made.
