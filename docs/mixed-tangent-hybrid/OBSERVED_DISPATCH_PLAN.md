# Model-declared tangent: observed qualification

## Question

On the already opened ESA recording 187, does choosing the tangent from the
physical model ID before a fit preserve the established wide-method fit and
prediction? This is a software substitution check, not an Earth-model test.

## Fixed cases and method

Reuse the exact prepared inputs, 800 earlier receiver positions, 400 later
positions, calibration bounds, starts, error policies and 200-evaluation
per-fit ceiling from the completed 24-case observed paired study. The model
rule is fixed before fitting: selective tangent for either globe model, wide
tangent for flat. The prior saved-result audit verifies the flat identity and
retains its one original numerical failure. Run fresh, same-source wide and
selected-method fits for the **16 globe cases**; do not rerun the eight flat
cases or change their saved dispositions. Use the earlier wide pilot only for
input and protocol binding, not as a same-source fit reference.

Each globe case has at most 402 native calls and 77,621,507,200 packet/query
visits. The 16-case ceiling is 6,432 calls and 1,241,944,115,200 visits.
Use four workers with one numerical thread each and a 30-hour cooperative
wall ceiling. A technical interruption may resume only unfinished work under
unchanged source, inputs, environment and finite separately charged limits;
sealed passes and failures never restart. Prove task-level reboot recovery
before launch. Preserve attempts, partial output and numerical failures.
No original aircraft file or protected ESA confirmation group may be opened.

## Gates

Before preparation, qualify the observed adapter, model-to-method mapping,
input hashes, frozen source/environment, training-only fit boundary, later
position isolation, all nuisance columns, fixed limits and reboot recovery.
Require the completed generated model-declared six-fit study and the
historical 24-case audit as bound parents. Then prepare a fresh identity and
record source, environment and finite work limits.

For each observed globe case, require both fits independently to meet the
existing numerical and physical checks. Compare all 400 later three-dimensional
positions at the same times; maximum matched difference must be at most
0.01 m. The two methods must retain the same numerical classification and
training-only primary-start choice. Publish every case, including failures.
Only a fully verified 16/16 globe comparison plus the saved eight-case flat
identity can qualify model-declared substitution over the 24-case development
matrix. Observed predictive adequacy, calibrated uncertainty and any physical
model decision remain separate gates.
