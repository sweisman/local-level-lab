# The endpoint-certificate change did not pass the runtime gate

**Only two of six timing pairs completed.** In each globe pair, one call reached
the unchanged 120-second ceiling. Both implementations were affected. The two
complete flat-disc pairs retained exact report agreement, but their time ratios
were 0.9673 and 1.0111; they do not demonstrate the required speed improvement.

The completed study used all twelve bound-call reservations and took 370.30
seconds. It cannot be retried or expanded. The earlier 24-case exact-report
qualification still stands, but software equivalence alone does not establish
runtime usefulness. No replacement future-bound rollout follows.

The saved source profile gives a more specific explanation: the two rotation
certificates consumed about 13.3 seconds of the 25.4-second physical-value
stream in its first case. Both rotation certificates remain necessary to
preserve midpoint and endpoint state. Omitting the endpoint force certificate
does not remove that work. Full prediction propagation contains additional
costs that the source-only profile did not measure.

Next is one separately bounded, whole-bound profile on the original generated
domain. It will retain timing even if the bound does not finish, distinguish
wall time from process CPU time, and identify the remaining costly operations.
It is a diagnostic, not a relaxed qualification or another aircraft fit.
