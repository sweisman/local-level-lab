# Saved-primary artifact inventory

Inventory the final six declared model/case primaries for each of the 53 selected
stretches: **318 identities**, once each. Follow the current verified disposition
ledger to its authoritative comparison. Never select a historical fit by cost,
substitute a repeat for a primary or treat a fixed-time profile as a full fit.

The batch permits at most 318 primary linearization reads and 318 epoch-artifact
reads, with no original recordings, native integration, optimizer starts, dense
factorizations or singular decompositions. It is a metadata/array inspection.
One batch, no retries after a charged start; rendering completion performs no
inspection. Freeze the selection, source metadata, software, environment and this
plan before the first primary inspection. Preserve missing and incompatible
artifact outcomes explicitly; do not repair a scientific artifact.

Check a declared artifact's SHA-256 against the authoritative fit. A published
copy named in that comparison's manifest may supply the same exact bytes. Verify
the full parameter order and point, residual cost, all Jacobian columns, receiver
position layout and saved epoch times against the frozen receiver selection.
For separate epoch artifacts, check that their relative times and selected UTC
times differ by one constant origin within eight floating-point ulps at their
recorded time scale. This is a metadata consistency check, not independent clock
calibration. Do not interpolate positions or alter times.

Distinguish a complete model comparison from reusable saved arrays. If a passing
primary has no saved linearization, record that fact; it does not reverse its
existing disposition. The inventory may expose array/metadata mismatches that
prevent reuse, and those must remain explicit. No missing artifact authorizes a
new prediction or fit. A later fixed-parameter projection audit needs its own
finite inventory, covariance/source freeze and operation limits.
