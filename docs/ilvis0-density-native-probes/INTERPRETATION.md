# The large linear examples fail the full training check

All twelve fixed probes completed correctly. **None satisfied the nonlinear
training constraints.** Their largest earlier-position changes were about
114–1,809 m, compared with the unchanged 1 m allowance.

This is useful: the large spreads in the simplified linear diagnostic have
not been demonstrated as genuine nonlinear prediction ambiguity. The proposed
calibrations can cancel in the linear formula while failing in the full model.
The later displacement played no role in rejecting them.

These are generated-data checks around the saved reference calibration. They
do not measure aircraft error, prove that every nonlinear prediction is narrow,
or permit unexamined calibration regions to be discarded.

The next saved-data audit will include the 24 shared source terms that the
linear-only diagnostic deliberately omitted. It will compare those predictions
with the already saved native values. Only matching coefficient maps may be
reused; old training-conditioned error bounds do not automatically apply to
these new points.
