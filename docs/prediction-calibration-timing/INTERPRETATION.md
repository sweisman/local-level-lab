# Calibration stopped at a numerical check

**348 of 7,176 planned starts returned results in 6.82 hours. Of these, 344
passed the numerical checks, two failed and two stopped cooperatively.**
The remaining starts were not run. No calibration threshold was established,
so independent validation was not launched.

All 346 completed forecasts met the unchanged position-error limits. These
are generated measurements: each candidate fits data generated for that same
candidate. They are neither additional aircraft observations nor votes for
an Earth model.

Both prescribed starts failed for the same generated case. They reached nearly
identical fitted values and later prediction errors: about 0.70 m horizontally
and 1.60 m vertically. However, eight of nine timing-cell verification probes
failed the required stability checks. Good prediction errors alone cannot
replace numerical qualification.

The saved records identify a specific diagnostic lead: those eight linear
verification proposals have higher predicted costs than their own anchor
points. The ninth proposal passes. The next step is to check the bounded linear
calculation and its handling of extremely narrow timing intervals. This is a
lead, not a demonstrated cause or permission to relabel the failed fits.

The complete cohort, original acceptance limits, failed results and unused
allowance remain closed. The 53-stretch airborne findings are unchanged.
