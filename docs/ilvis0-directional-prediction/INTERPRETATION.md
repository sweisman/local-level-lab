# The prediction ranges did not improve

The study reached its 40-minute cooperative ceiling. It preserves 39 passing
sector calculations, four interrupted starts and five unstarted sectors. No
process was killed and no allowance was extended. The study is closed; its
unfinished work will not be retried under this allowance.

All four globe candidate/processing groups completed their eight-sector
comparisons. Their combined prediction ranges are exactly the same as the
inherited ranges. Both specified-field groups still miss the fixed width target.
Completing the remaining disc sectors cannot change that failed all-group gate.
The wider fresh ranges remain visible in the complete report.

The [saved baseline audit](BASELINE_DIAGNOSIS.md) explains the main limitation.
In all 39 completed cases, the existing training-derived acceleration bound is
tighter than the new physical-only bound in every component and in vector norm.
The promising static comparison used a broader baseline than the prediction
method actually used. That comparison was insufficient grounds to expect a
complete improvement.

The static and native controls remain valid component results. The next method
must preserve the training information and address direction loss in dynamic
propagation. No aircraft fit, measurement or published 53-stretch finding changed.
