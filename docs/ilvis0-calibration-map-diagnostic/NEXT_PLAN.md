# Couple calibration errors before more prediction fitting

The extreme-point diagnostic establishes a representation problem, not a new
prediction interval. More minor matrix inequalities are no longer the default.

## V01: next method to qualify

Develop a prediction treatment in which all 24 common-error terms are functions
of the same 27 physical calibration values. First use independent, cheap nonlinear
controls with known feasible regions, nonunique calibration and boundary optima.
Require source binding, complete coordinates, error terms, failure preservation
and charge-before-evaluation accounting.

The [coupled-map implementation](../ilvis0-coupled-map-controls/README.md) now has
checked interval arithmetic, all 27 first derivatives and necessary mean-value
relationships. Next connect the complete static source map, qualify its value
and derivative bounds, then freeze the new comparison. The primitives alone do
not satisfy this gate.

A local constrained solve can supply feasible examples and reveal important
directions. It cannot supply a global upper bound or calibrated uncertainty.
Any claimed prediction range needs a justified nonlinear enclosure or separately
validated statistical construction, with training observations alone determining
calibration and error assumptions. Known generated anchors are forbidden as
prediction inputs. Preserve the existing width target until a replacement design
is explicitly justified and frozen before seeing its outcomes.

Then freeze a finite comparison across all six candidate/processing groups,
including all original orientation sectors. Test both containment and usefulness
against the sealed generated histories. No new aircraft prediction follows until
the method's numerical and adequacy gates pass.
