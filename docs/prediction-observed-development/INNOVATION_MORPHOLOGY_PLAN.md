# Saved training-error pattern audit

Use only the two sealed 600-report ESA development fits. Verify their saved
whitened residuals against the fit cost and published lag-1, lag-5 and lag-20
training summary. Describe each north, east and height error series with
shifted correlations at lags 1–120, same-time cross-axis correlations, and
the fraction of mean-removed spectral power at periods longer than 60 and
120 receiver reports. The reports arrive about one second apart; use report
index as the lag coordinate and retain actual min/max spacing as provenance.

As a compact descriptive parameterization, solve an exponential-plus-white
correlation curve from height lags 1 and 20 and compare its predicted lag 5
with the observed lag 5. Do not claim that those two components are uniquely
identified physical sensor processes. The fit itself also shapes residuals.

No optimization, original measurement read, later receiver read or reserved
flight group. The scientific decision stays abstain. The audit informs
independently generated error-law adversaries; it cannot qualify a forecast
range or decide between Earth models.
