# Saved forecast-horizon audit

Question: In the completed twelve-recording generated comparison, does the
distance between candidate receiver-forecast centers grow faster than their
whole-path range widths at later withheld times?

Use only sealed artifacts from the completed study. For each candidate pair
and each of the 40 later receiver epochs, express center separation and the
sum of coordinate half-widths in a local metric fixed at the last training
receiver position. Record their horizontal ratio and whether any coordinate
ranges are disjoint. Summarize epochs 1, 10, 20 and 40 across all twelve
recordings, preserving each recording's values in a machine-readable report.

This is descriptive saved-data diagnosis. It may explain why the finite
development matrix abstained, but cannot calibrate aircraft uncertainty,
select an Earth model or authorize refitting. It uses zero new fit starts and
opens no observed or reserved aircraft data.
