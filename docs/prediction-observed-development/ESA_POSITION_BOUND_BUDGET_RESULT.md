The trace reproduced every archived bound and packet-work count. At 9.55
seconds, the accumulated residual-velocity allowance accounts for 98% of
the globe's traced departure remainder and 99% of the disc's, on the
largest horizontal axis for each candidate.

The initial offset and arithmetic difference round to zero in both rows.
Reconstruction errors across all axes and checkpoints were below 10⁻¹⁵ m.
The table traces the accumulated departure. The full bounds also include
the separate reference force and receiver lever-arm terms. At 9.55 seconds,
those terms alone exceed the 4.03 cm uniform target on the largest axis in
both candidates. Tightening velocity alone cannot meet that target in this
calculation. Scaling the work to the full history would leave these width
problems in place.

Both tasks completed in 184.7 seconds. Ten related tests passed. The run
used two bound charges and no fits, new receiver measurements or reserved
flights. These are contributions to conservative bounds. They do not give
the actual errors in either forecast.
