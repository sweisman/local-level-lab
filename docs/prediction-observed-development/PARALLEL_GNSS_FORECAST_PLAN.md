# Score the sealed ESA forecast against parallel receivers

The two 400/120 IMU fits and their early-only conditioning tasks are already sealed. Their later 120 `air1` scores are public. This new development check asks whether the conditioned forecast has comparable **position error** against the other two physical GNSS receiver exports from the same opened flight (recording 187). It was designed after seeing the `air1` outcome, so it is not independent statistical validation.

Reverify the sealed fit and conditioning manifests, their source and input hashes, and the already published `air1` score. Use the saved conditional receiver means unchanged. Match the next 120 receiver times to the `mosaic03` and `pwrpak7` PPP exports within 2 ms, once each and without interpolation. Before scoring, bring each observed position to the `air1` forecast epoch using its reported north/east/up velocity times the signed timestamp offset and the local WGS84 coordinate radii. Preserve the raw times, source hashes and original positions. Do not fit a new model or law, alter the forecast, open another flight group or use a receiver observation to correct the forecast.

For each of two saved IMU error laws and three receivers, report horizontal and height RMS errors, component mean errors and the difference from the `air1` score. Reproduce the existing `air1` score with the same scoring function before accepting the additional comparisons. No detection, coverage or absolute GNSS accuracy claim follows: the receivers share an antenna and may share PPP processing, and all results are from one previously opened development flight.

Finite work: at most 120 future epochs × three receiver streams × two sealed laws, zero new fits or native calculations, one numerical thread. The earlier [parallel-source report](PARALLEL_GNSS_RESULT.json) fixes filenames and hashes. [The original scored forecast](ROLLING_ORIGIN_400_CONDITIONAL_RESULT.json) fixes sealed artifact identities, training boundary and baseline. New source and runtime hashes are recorded in the result. The seven reserved ESA groups stay closed.
