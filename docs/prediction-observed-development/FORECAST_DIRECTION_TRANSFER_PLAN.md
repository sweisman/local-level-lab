# Can a forecast direction learned on another path transfer?

Use the twelve sealed generated histories with three fitted model forecasts
each. Recover the earlier-only forecast centers and verify their saved
hashes. For each target history, compare two fixed choices of training
histories. Use all five other histories on the same generated route, or
use all six histories from the other route. Each target seed remains out
of its own direction construction. Use all four existing covariance
assumptions and the common 40-report support.

For each donor form its two forecast contrasts against the specified-field
globe. Normalize each in the target's declared covariance metric and average
the corresponding directions. Check the span of these two means against
the target's three actual pair distances. Also report the least information
retained over any combination of the target contrasts. Repeat with cubic
position trends removed. These are directional transfer measures, without
later receiver outcomes or a decision threshold.

Use one thread, 48 error operators and 192 comparisons of donor and target
spaces. Reuse only the earlier fitted paths. Allow no new fits, new random
draws, later receiver observations or protected flight reads. Results will
inform whether fixed external directions are realistic for the next
method design.
