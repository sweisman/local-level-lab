# Correction: generated-fit accuracy limit

The generated complete-fit code inherited its physical accuracy limits from
`airquantumgrav_training_controls.recipe()`: **0.1 m horizontal RMS, 0.1 m
vertical RMS and 0.5 m maximum three-dimensional error**, each required in
both training and later sections. The full-fit innovation study, bound-aware
pilot and current eight-case generated cohort use that same recipe. Their
source-bound plans and some historical reports describe a “10 m horizontal
RMS screen.” That wording is incorrect. Those records remain preserved as
written; this note supplies the correction.

The previously reported 91–219 m later horizontal misses fail both 0.1 m
and 10 m, so their failure labels do not change. For the current cohort, the
technical task outcome is separate from physical accuracy. Its report must
show raw errors and the **actual coded limits**, and must not claim that a
physical pass or failure was established under a different rule. Any result
between 0.1 m and 10 m requires an explicit discrepancy label rather than
a silently revised decision.

This correction applies only to the named generated complete-fit studies.
Other observed-aircraft studies may have different frozen limits.
