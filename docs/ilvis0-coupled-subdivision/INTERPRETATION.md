# Smaller boxes help only slightly

All 192 calculations pass their correctness checks. Every predeclared region is
included, and the known generated trajectories remain inside their prediction
ranges. The calculation preserves the original physical assumptions.

The ranges narrow by at most 1.26% compared with the parent calculation. Four of
six candidate/processing groups meet the unchanged target; both globe groups
with the specified background field still miss it. Their east and height ranges
remain too wide.

This tests a generated 7.2-second trajectory, not an aircraft recording. It shows
that splitting the two tested mounting-angle coordinates is insufficient. It
does not change the 53-stretch findings or qualify full-flight prediction errors.
No new aircraft fits or native trajectory calculations ran.
