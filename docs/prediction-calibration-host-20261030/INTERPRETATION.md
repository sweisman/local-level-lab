# Prediction calibration: a complete result

**All 7,176 simulated fits passed their numerical checks and the fixed
position-error checks.** The campaign finished in 151.7 hours. Independent
checks reproduced the saved results and then reproduced the report from its
publication copies. No fit was rerun for either check.

The fits belong to **299 independent simulated cases**, each with 24 prescribed
settings and starting points. Those related fits are not separate independent
trials. All cases returned usable results, with no interruptions.

## What this adds

Earlier campaigns stopped before they could set a usable prediction-error
limit. This campaign supplies that limit: **27.6605 on the predefined normalized
forecast-error score**. The score measures prediction error relative to the
declared noise scale; the limit is not a distance in metres.

The calibration uses an order-statistic rule targeting 99% coverage with 95%
confidence under the frozen simulation population. That statement depends on
the simulation's error assumptions. It does not establish error coverage for
actual aircraft or statistical significance for the Earth-model comparison.

## What comes next

An independent test uses **181 new simulated cases and 4,344 prescribed fits**.
It keeps the calibrated limit fixed. Its separate requirement is at least 95%
coverage with 95% confidence; the frozen plan permits at most four failed case
families. No validation results have been used to choose the limit.

Exact results, failures, source versions, inputs and work charges are preserved
in verified archives. Duplicate working copies have been compacted. The current
independent-test phase is reported on the execution-status page below.
