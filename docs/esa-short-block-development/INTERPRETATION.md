# How to interpret the short-block selection

The identities of the three additional twenty-minute windows were fixed from
receiver metadata before their model outcomes were examined. The later
**120-report prediction length was chosen after the first development
window's 400-report forecast and shorter-prefix scores were known**. The
generated covariance calculation also informed the choice.

This makes the 120-report design an exploratory development revision. Results
from these three windows can test feasibility and expose failures, but they
cannot serve as independent confirmation of the revised duration or establish
calibrated coverage. All three are from one flight group. The earlier
400-report failure stays in the record. Independent confirmation requires a
separately frozen method and the still-reserved flight groups.

The [frozen selection](README.md) records exact window indices and hashes.
Its receipt remains unchanged; this note clarifies the order in which the
design decision and earlier outcome inspection occurred.
