# Regrouped orientation-cover usefulness comparison

The new native construction passes all 24 qualification identities. Compare it
with the completed orientation cover using the **same byte-identical generated
400-packet inputs**, original physical bounds, all three candidates, both
processing cases and eight closed yaw sectors. Preserve all earlier results.

Keep the same six training times, 1.80-second prediction, 1 mm declared training
allowance, 10 micrometre arithmetic allowance and 1 micrometre center check.
Use the qualified regrouped native bounds in place of independent packet
remainders. Retain every sector not excluded by a checked necessary constraint.
An LP failure cannot discard a sector. Evaluate future truth only after the
sector's training region, center and future bounds are fixed.

Report the full union for each of the six cases, including worse or unchanged
ranges. Show ratios to **both** the original broad-box result and the previous
orientation cover. Preserve the original component target: every axis in every
case must be at least ten times narrower than the original broad-box baseline.
Correctness requires all known parameters to survive their sector's necessary
constraints and every future truth to remain in its complete union. Even meeting
this short-control target is not recording-scale qualification.

Save all native values and 27-column Jacobians, training observations and
inequalities, coordinate scales, chosen domains/centers, future derivatives,
support certificates and bound terms. These enable subsequent diagnosis without
repeating native evaluations. Hash every side artifact through its result.

Freeze **48 identities / 192 native evaluations / 76,800 native packet visits**,
at most **384 bounds / 153,600 reserved bound packet steps**, and at most 288
one-thread LP proposals / 576 exact support certificates. Charge before each
operation. One worker reserves four numerical threads. New qualification timings
are 1.75–2.07 seconds per broad-case bound plus its native probes; allow a separate
**900-second cooperative ceiling**, approximately eight minutes expected.
This is a new study's allowance, not an expansion of any completed study.
No retries, extensions, process signals, observed fits or original-file reads.

Verify the complete qualification receipts, unchanged native/clock sources and
all predecessor input hashes before launch. The source/input/environment freeze
must include the old comparison and the new execution/serialization code.
Partial or failed studies remain terminal and published. A failed correctness
gate blocks dependent work; a correct but broad result blocks qualified
observational predictions. Actual error-law adequacy, global numerical treatment
and independent confirmation remain separate gates. Protected ESA groups remain
sealed.
