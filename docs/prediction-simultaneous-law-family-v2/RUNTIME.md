# Saved runtime audit

This audit uses the closed study summaries. It performs no fit and changes no
earlier outcome.

| Allowance | Finished tasks | Elapsed | Outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Original eight-hour study | 84 of 96 | 8 h 0 min | 80 passed; four stopped at the deadline |
| Separate continuation | 12 of 12 previously unstarted | 1 h 0 min | 12 passed |

Each recording reserved 479,632 native calls and 3,012,102,024 packet visits.
The four stopped recordings had used their charged per-task work and remain
sealed technical failures. The continuation did not repeat them.

The combined wall time was about nine hours using four workers of one numerical
thread each. This explains why the original eight-hour cap was insufficient for
this 96-recording workload; it does not show that a longer cap would change an
individual fit or interval result. A fresh cohort needs its own seed stream,
fixed allowance and qualification before launch.
