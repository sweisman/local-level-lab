# Prediction calculation: improvement, but not qualification

The study finished in 81.3 minutes. **32 of 48 calibration-region calculations
passed; 16 reached the existing per-bound time limit.** All failures, charges
and partial artifacts are preserved. The completed study will not be retried.

Three of the six model/processing groups have every region available. For the
two specified-field globe groups, combining the new and earlier valid ranges
reduces height widths by 25–28%. Horizontal widths change by only 0–2.5% and
still miss the original target. The complete zero-field/zero-removal globe
group passes that target. The other three groups remain incomplete.

The earlier qualified comparison still passes four of six groups. This study
does not replace missing regions with successful ones or turn partial ranges
into complete results. It adds useful height bounds, but does **not** qualify
the full method for withheld aircraft predictions.

These checks use six generated 7.2-second histories. They add no aircraft fits
and leave the 53-stretch findings unchanged. Numerical error bounds,
full-recording performance and calibrated observational uncertainty remain
separate requirements.

Next: identify the costly propagation operations and examine the remaining
horizontal error terms from saved artifacts before changing the method.
Keep the physical bounds, failed results and prediction target unchanged.
