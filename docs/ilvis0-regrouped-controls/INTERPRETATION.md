# Regrouped propagation passes its qualification

All **24 generated identities pass** across the three physical candidates and
both processing cases. The construction preserves every one of the 27 nuisance
directions. It combines force and gravity before their common duration is
bounded and uses one correlated clock for the reference position advance.

- All **162 tangent-column checks** pass. The largest finite-difference
  discrepancy is 13.7 micrometres per normalized parameter, below the frozen
  20 micrometre limit.
- Centers agree with the unchanged native engine to within 8.9 nanometres in
  these fixtures. That is numerical agreement, not instrument accuracy.
- All packet-boundary and broad-region probes stay within their Taylor bounds.
- All six independently generated stationary-pose controls retain the known
  fixed position, including nonzero field/removal cases.

The study spent 78 native calls and 348 bound evaluations in 45.8 seconds.
Every generated input, native output/Jacobian and bound is preserved. The
completed freeze cannot be restarted or expanded.

This qualifies the finite generated comparison. It does not yet show useful
long-record uncertainty, certify all floating-point behavior or establish the
actual receiver-error law. The next separate experiment repeats the complete
orientation-cover comparison with the same original inputs, physical limits,
sectors and improvement target. It will preserve the earlier broad results
and save its training constraints for subsequent metadata-only diagnosis.
