# Participant protocol

The same text appears in the app (Instructions). The source of truth is `android/app/src/main/java/io/github/sweisman/locallevellab/ui/Instructions.kt`. Keep the two in sync.

1. **Before you fly.** Charge the phone and bring a mount or tape. Book a window seat if you can.
2. **Pre-flight calibration (~20 min).** Put the phone on a solid table, edge against a straight edge, in 4 positions: screen up, screen up turned 180°, screen down, screen down turned 180°. About 5 still minutes each. Don't touch anything.
3. **Optional drift run.** 30+ minutes lying still, for example overnight.
4. **Place the phone.** Best is a window frame or sidewall mount. Good is clamped to the seat structure. Acceptable is taped on a locked tray (cruise only). Never hold it. The orientation doesn't matter as long as it stays fixed. Run the placement check (60 s stable).
5. **Record the flight.** Airplane mode on, Location on. Record as much of the cruise as you can. Battery-saver mode is fine. Tap "I moved the phone" if it gets bumped.
6. **Post-flight calibration.** The same 4 positions, as soon as you can after landing.
7. **Upload or share.** Uploads happen only when you tap Upload.

**Controls:**

- Turn the phone 180° in its mount on another flight, or halfway through a long one.
- Mount two phones in different orientations.
- Ground-only stationary recordings test Earth rotation at your latitude.

Always follow crew instructions and airline rules on devices.
