## Saved-record and tangent audit

[audit.json](audit.json), generated by [audit_geometry.py](../../analysis/tests/audit_geometry.py),
preserves the historical freeze and reports a new, separately identified analytic audit. No
flight, optimizer or bootstrap runs are used by that audit. It found:

- Nuisance SVD rank depended on parameter units because weighted columns were not normalized.
  Independent column rescaling changed a fixture's science rank from one to two. Unit-column
  normalization fixes this, including extreme scales and zero columns; the gate cutoff is unchanged.
- Six-heading 60-minute fixtures give every leg exactly ten minutes, equal to the cruise minimum.
  Turn smoothing can disqualify intermediate legs: the +35° control retains only 25 bins and
  two of six headings even without watchdog exclusions. Qualifying dwell must exceed the minimum.
- All six no-fit records have `mount_slip_detected`, `magnetic_yaw_change_ambiguous` and `no_bins`:
  watchdog selection removed every bin. Do not reinterpret them as optimizer failures or remove gates.
- The 180-minute/six-heading/three-turn analytic fixture stays rank zero with all headings available;
  removing dynamic bias diagnostically restores rank two at all anchors. Slow signals can be absorbed
  by unpenalized 15-minute bias knots, so longer duration alone does not establish better design.
- All six exact-control wind cases change worst-anchor rank under a ±10% cutoff shift. Seven of
  300 evaluations change worst-anchor rank overall. A subthreshold direction is discarded, making
  contrast retention discontinuous; new reports show pre-cutoff information alongside retained values.
- Wind Jacobians agree with finite differences at all three anchors. Removing crab, bias or forward
  nuisance directions can improve analytic information; these ablations do not justify relaxing gates.

Each case runs in a child process, bounded by the smaller of 300 seconds and the remaining
authorized time. A 300-second timeout is a completed computational failure, never rerun for
better acceptance. The final budget-limited incomplete case is not counted as completed.
The parent stops and terminates the child at the deadline; termination/IO cleanup can add
a few seconds. Reservations are persisted before execution and settled after journal fsync.
An abrupt crash conservatively charges the entire reserved slice (at most five minutes),
so resuming cannot silently refill the budget. Pauses between workers do not consume time.
Source/environment/input/runner changes prevent resume and are checked between every case.
Missing original journals require recovery, not restarting from an empty campaign.

The default for future work is up to two total compute threads. This completed
batch retains its frozen one-thread environment. New two-thread campaigns need a new
environment manifest and timing measurement; calibration and validation must use the same freeze.
