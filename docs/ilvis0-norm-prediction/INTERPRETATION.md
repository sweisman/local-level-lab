passed. Complete prediction widths narrowed by less than 0.1% relative to the
preceding calibration-consistency calculation. Only three of six model/processing
groups meet the unchanged twofold target on every axis.

The extra constraints are valid but have little effect on this prediction
construction. More directional facets of the same vector balls are a low
priority. The calculation took 36.9 seconds and performed no native integration,
aircraft fitting or original-file reads. Earlier evidence remains unchanged.

arbitrarily along their own directions. A separate component qualification and
