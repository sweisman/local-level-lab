# Shared errors survive the native-model checks

All **24 generated controls pass**: the three physical models, both processing
cases, and tangent, packet-boundary, broad-calibration and independently generated
stationary-motion families. All 162 physical tangent-column checks pass. The
largest native center discrepancy is 9.86 nanometres. The study used its 78
charged native evaluations and has finished; its inputs, source and results are
sealed.

The calculation now preserves two nonlinear errors common to a recording:
initial velocity times the common clock offset, and the initial attitude/lever
product. The original 27 physical calibration directions remain. Six additional
bounded error coordinates represent those common errors; they are not new
physical freedoms or six new choices at each measurement time.

The next check connects all measurement times to **one common six-coordinate
block**, then repeats the complete orientation-cover usefulness comparison. A
short native-control pass does not establish useful long-record prediction,
whole-program floating-point enclosure, or actual receiver-error coverage.
Observed prediction studies remain behind those gates. The 53-stretch model
comparison and the protected independent ESA recordings are unchanged.
