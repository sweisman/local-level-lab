# Direct-source comparison: narrower ranges, incomplete coverage

**36 of 48 regions pass; 12 reach the per-bound time limit.** All 48 outcomes,
336 charged bounds and their frozen inputs and sources are preserved. The
study finishes in 84.9 minutes and does not qualify the full six-group matrix.
It cannot be restarted or donate unused allowance.

Three groups have all eight regions complete: globe with zero field and
fixed-zero removal, and both flat-disc processing cases. Their fresh north /
east / height widths are respectively:

| Complete group | Widths in metres | Original target |
|---|---:|---|
| Globe, zero field / zero removal | 0.7666 / 0.7998 / 0.5658 | Pass |
| Flat disc / zero removal | 0.6459 / 0.2002 / 0.3924 | Pass |
| Flat disc / profiled removal | 0.7440 / 0.2370 / 0.4865 | Pass |

Both specified-field globe groups and the zero-field globe with profiled
removal remain incomplete. Their partial regions do not define a complete
range and cannot qualify those groups. Every failed task reached the time
limit on its seventh, future-bound call; its six completed training bounds
remain distinct saved artifacts.

These are generated 7.2-second software cases. No aircraft recording, native
trajectory oracle or optimizer was used. The 53 empirical model comparisons
are unchanged. A narrow interval here is not a measured aircraft error or a
calibrated confidence interval.

## Next gate

Check compatible saved certificates before repeating integration. A preliminary
inspection finds matching earlier future bounds and newer completed training
bounds for all 16 specified-field regions. Their reuse needs its own frozen
policy, known-answer controls and support allowance. It cannot change either
original study's failed labels or permit a partial new union. If that approach
does not meet the target, profile and qualify an implementation improvement.

Recording-scale error bounds, floating-execution assumptions and observational
error assessment remain ahead of withheld-flight fitting.
