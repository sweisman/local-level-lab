**In plain words.** Before the method is tested on the reserved ESA flights, it needs a stated model of how wrong the aircraft's measurements could be. This page sets out what that model has to cover: GPS errors shared across the aircraft's receivers, errors in the motion sensor, and uncertainty in the fitted corrections. Ground stations help with some shared GPS effects on the development flight. Faster errors and effects at the aircraft's antenna remain open. The page also records checks on the software that calculates the limits.
