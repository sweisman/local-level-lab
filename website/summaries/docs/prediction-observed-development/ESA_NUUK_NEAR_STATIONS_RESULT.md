**In plain words.** Two ground stations about 1 km apart should see almost the same satellite ranges, so their disagreement shows how noisy such a reference is. Here they differed by 6.49 mm RMS, on the 100 seconds that passed the quality checks. That is disagreement between two stations. It does not tell us the error of either station alone, or of the aircraft.

*Background: one two-minute comparison on the ESA development flight scores the competing Earth models using millimetre-level satellite-range measurements. "The gap" is the difference between their scores.*
