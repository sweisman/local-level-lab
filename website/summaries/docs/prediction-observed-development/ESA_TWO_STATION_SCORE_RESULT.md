**In plain words.** Could one GPS error, shared by the aircraft and the ground stations, account for the whole gap? This page uses two ground stations to test it. A shared error that large would require at least one station to carry 5.43 mm RMS of error of its own. The data alone do not put an upper limit on either station's error, so this narrows the possibility without excluding it.

*Background: one two-minute comparison on the ESA development flight scores the competing Earth models using millimetre-level satellite-range measurements. "The gap" is the difference between their scores.*
