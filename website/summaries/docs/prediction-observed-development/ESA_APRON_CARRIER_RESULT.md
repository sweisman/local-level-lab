**In plain words.** A parked aircraft does not move, so its GPS ranges should match a fixed position. That makes a ground stop a direct test of the aircraft's own GPS installation. On an 18.5-second excerpt of the stop at Nuuk, the ranges disagreed with a fixed position by about 7 mm RMS. Moving the assumed position by five metres changed that figure by less than 0.025 mm. A first run stopped on a clock setting and is kept in the record with the corrected one.
