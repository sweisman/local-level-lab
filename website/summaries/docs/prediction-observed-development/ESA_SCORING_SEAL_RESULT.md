**In plain words.** If forecasts were chosen after seeing how well they scored, the result would be biased. This page replays the ESA development test and confirms the order of operations: each forecast was chosen by how well its fit matched the earlier data, and only then was the score summary opened. All 24 tasks were checked and all 10 available forecasts reproduced. This verifies the procedure. It does not show that the forecasts are accurate enough, which needs independent validation.
