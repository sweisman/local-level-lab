**In plain words.** On one ESA development flight, each model was fitted to 400 GPS reports and then had to predict the next 120, which it was not shown. In the two settings where every fit completed, the globe with the specified field was far closer: its horizontal error was about 5 m, against 2,590 m for the globe without the field and 3,997 m for the flat disc. In the other two settings the disc fits did not converge, so those comparisons are unresolved. This is one development flight, not a confirmation.
