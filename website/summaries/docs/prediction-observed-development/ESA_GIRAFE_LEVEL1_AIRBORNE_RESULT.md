**In plain words.** A second instrument on the ESA flight, called GIRAFE, recorded force independently. Its data come in two files, original and processed. This page checks that the two line up in time. Their vertical readings match exactly at the same GPS times in both airborne intervals, with the sign reversed in the processed file. That settles how to match the files. It adds no new measurement, and the timing and accuracy of the horizontal channels remain open.
