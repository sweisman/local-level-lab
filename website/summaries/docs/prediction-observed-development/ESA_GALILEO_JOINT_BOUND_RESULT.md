**In plain words.** This page adds the Galileo measurements to the broader limits on flight-path error. They change little: the existing GPS, reference-position and motion limits do most of the work, and loosening the Galileo limit from 8 mm to 1 m barely moves the result. Two reduced GPS satellite sets remain open under the example limits. The Galileo limit used is illustrative.

*Background: one two-minute comparison on the ESA development flight scores the competing Earth models using millimetre-level satellite-range measurements. "The gap" is the difference between their scores.*
