**In plain words.** With the aircraft parked at Nuuk, its GPS errors can be compared with those of the nearby Nuuk Museum station. If both were dominated by the same satellite errors, the two would rise and fall together. They did not: the correlation was 0.014, and subtracting the station's errors made the aircraft's figure worse, from 7.03 to 9.46 mm RMS. So on this short excerpt there is little sign that one shared error dominates.
