**In plain words.** GPS antennas shift the measured range slightly depending on the direction of the satellite, and published corrections exist for satellite and ground-station antennas. This page applies them. They changed the score by 0.0012% of the gap, which is negligible. The aircraft's own antenna is not identified in its files, so its effect is not covered here.

*Background: one two-minute comparison on the ESA development flight scores the competing Earth models using millimetre-level satellite-range measurements. "The gap" is the difference between their scores.*
