**In plain words.** The two GPS receivers on flight RF04 reported positions about 89 m apart along the track. This page asks why. Treating the difference as a timing offset of 0.39 s, learned from the first part of the stretch, cut the later difference from 89 m to 0.49 m RMS. That was the best of the three explanations tested. Across the direction of flight the two tracks were already within about 0.2 m.
