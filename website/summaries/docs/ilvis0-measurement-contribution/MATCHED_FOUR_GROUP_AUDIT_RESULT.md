**In plain words.** A forecast test fits a model to the early part of a flight and asks it to predict GPS positions later in the same flight, which it was not shown. On four NASA flight groups, 15 of 16 fits gave a forecast that could be scored. With the same background field for both shapes, the globe's forecast had lower 3D position error on all four flights. Three of the four globe forecasts met every position limit, and the fourth exceeded the 10 m horizontal limit. These are look-back tests on the original archive, not independent confirmation.
