**In plain words.** The aircraft file reports speed and course rounded to fixed steps. This page asks whether that rounding explains the disagreement between the two GPS receivers on flight RF04. On this nearly southbound stretch, a small rounding of course changes the east speed much more than the north speed. Rounding is large enough to account for every east disagreement tested. Some of the north disagreements are larger than rounding allows, so part remains unexplained.
