**In plain words.** This compares two records of the same flight stretch: the survey instrument's gyros and the aircraft's own navigation record. Over whole seconds, their measures of the aircraft's turning differ by 0.048° RMS, a typical size of the difference. The two GPS receivers report positions a steady 89 m apart, mostly along the direction of flight, which a later check traces to a timing difference. This checks the recording. It is not a test of any Earth model.
