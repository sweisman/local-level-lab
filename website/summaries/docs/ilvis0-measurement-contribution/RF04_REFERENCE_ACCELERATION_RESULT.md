**In plain words.** The force check depends on knowing how the aircraft accelerated, which comes from GPS. This page asks whether the two GPS receivers on flight RF04 agree. They differ by 0.075 m/s² RMS over two seconds and 0.020 m/s² RMS over ten seconds, almost all of it in the east direction. No Earth model enters this comparison. Two later checks look for the cause of the east disagreement.
