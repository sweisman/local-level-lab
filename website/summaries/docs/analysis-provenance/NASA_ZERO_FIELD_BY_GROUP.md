**In plain words.** The main comparison gives the globe a background rotation field and the disc none. This page asks a different question: what happens when neither shape has the field? In that case the globe fits better on 35 stretches and the disc on 18, and those 18 fall in 10 of the 27 recording groups. Neither shape fits the recordings well without the field, so this ordering carries little weight. With the field, the globe has the lowest cost on all 53. The page also groups stretches that share a flight or instrument, so they are not counted as separate evidence.
