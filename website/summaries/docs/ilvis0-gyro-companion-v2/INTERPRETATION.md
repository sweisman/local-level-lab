**In plain words.** Each model predicts a pitch rate for a stretch from its position, speed and direction alone. This page compares those predictions with the pitch rate the gyro actually recorded, with nothing fitted. Across the 53 stretches, the recorded rates lie closer to the globe with the specified field than to the globe without it or the flat disc, by the median measure used. Some stretches still differ substantially from the prediction. This is a descriptive check, not a fitted result.
