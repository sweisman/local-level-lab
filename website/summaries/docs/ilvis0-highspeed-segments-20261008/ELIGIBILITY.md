**In plain words.** These are the rules that chose which parts of the NASA flights were analysed. A stretch needs at least four continuous minutes of complete data with GPS ground speed of 700 km/h or more throughout, plus checks for steady flight. The rules look at data quality, duration, speed and motion, not at how well any Earth model fits. Applied to all 232 files, they give 53 stretches totalling 424.3 minutes. Different recorders on one flight can overlap in time, so these are not 53 independent flights.
